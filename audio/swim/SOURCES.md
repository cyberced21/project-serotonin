# Swimming sound sources

Picked by ear on the swimming sounds page (plans/water.md, 2026-10-31). **CC0** Freesound
recordings (the site's previews). The one-shots are cut and levelled to -20 dB RMS by
`tools/cut_dog_sounds.py` (kinds dive, gasp, climb_out); the two loops are the previews as they
are. Levels are in `SwimSounds` (scripts/swim_sounds.gd).
