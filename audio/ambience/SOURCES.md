# Ambience sources

Picked by ear on the ambience page (plans/cozy-sound.md, phase 8). **CC0** Freesound field
recordings (the site's 128 kbps previews, kept as MP3). Played by the `Ambience` autoload and
`WaterBody` through `LoopBed` (seamless loops: two players crossfading over the seam). Each file's
level fix is `Ambience.TRIM`, its place in the mix `Ambience.MIX`.
