# Tavern sound sources

**Provisional** (2026-10-29): first choices from the candidates on the Sleepy Hound Sounds page
(https://claude.ai/artifact/SsVfYHdugLLt1r2gyZyHsZ, plans/tavern.md phase 8), until the picks by ear
come in. Every one is a **CC0** Freesound recording (the site's previews), cut to its loudest
stretch and levelled to -20 dB RMS by `tools/cut_dog_sounds.py`. Levels: `Tavern.MURMUR_DB` /
`FIRE_DB`, the villager's own player for laugh and clink, `DiceGame.DICE_DB`.
