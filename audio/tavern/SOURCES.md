# Tavern sound sources

Picked by ear on the Sleepy Hound Sounds page (https://claude.ai/artifact/SsVfYHdugLLt1r2gyZyHsZ,
plans/tavern.md phase 8), 2026-10-29. Every one is a **CC0** Freesound recording (the site's
previews), cut to its loudest stretch and levelled to -20 dB RMS by `tools/cut_dog_sounds.py`.
Levels: `Tavern.MURMUR_DB` / `FIRE_DB`, the villager's own player for laugh and clink,
`DiceGame.DICE_DB`.
