# Moment cue sources

Every cue is a **CC0** recording (public domain, no credit required), picked by ear on the cue
page (plans/cozy-sound.md, phase 3). Played by `Music.play_cue(id)`; levels in
`data/music/library.tres` (`trim_db`, measured with `tools/measure_music.gd -- --dir=res://audio/cues/`).

| File | Cue | Title | Author | Source |
|------|-----|-------|--------|--------|
| level_up.ogg | a skill levels up | Win Jingle (Strings variation) | Fupi | https://opengameart.org/content/win-jingle |
| discovery.mp3 | a biome found for the first time | Success Fanfare Trumpets | FunWithSound | https://freesound.org/people/FunWithSound/sounds/456966/ |
| return.mp3 | back in a biome you know | Echo Chime Chime | husky70 | https://freesound.org/people/husky70/sounds/161316/ |
| upgrade.mp3 | a building goes up a tier | Level win | Tuudurt | https://freesound.org/people/Tuudurt/sounds/258142/ |

The Freesound files are the site's 128 kbps previews; the originals (needs a Freesound login)
can replace them.
