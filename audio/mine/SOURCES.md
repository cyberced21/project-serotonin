# Mine sound sources

plans/mine.md phase 1), 2026-10-29. Every one is a **CC0** Freesound recording (the site's
previews), cut to its loudest stretch and levelled to -20 dB RMS by `tools/cut_dog_sounds.py`
(the mine's kinds); the long ones then made 128 kbps MP3s.
