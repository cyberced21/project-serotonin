Hearthvale, redrawn

Three ways to lay out the bigger valley, and three ways to lay out the village green at its heart. Every sheet is drawn to scale from the game's own numbers; pick one map and one green.

The valley · 768 × 768 m

Three maps

All three keep what we agreed: the village at the middle, the places in their old compass directions and further out, the Pinewood and Goldmeadow about half again bigger, the quarry, Stillwater and Frostpeak their own size. They differ in how the land and the water run, and where the new places sit. Times are jogging (5.5 m/s) from the green, along the roads.

The village · about 140 m across

Three greens

The eight buildings you know (dark) and the new ones (red outline): farm buildings, stores and yards, the washhouse and chapel, the empty houses. Footprints are each building's top tier; the dotted squares are room left for add-ons.
