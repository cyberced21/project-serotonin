# The world pass (after cozy)

Grilled 2026-10-29. Umbrella: [cozy.md](cozy.md), "After cozy": the world feels too tight and too
sparse, rocks sit out of place, the village looks randomly laid out. Branch `world-pass` off `main`.

## The map

- **768 x 768 m** (3 x 3 Terrain3D regions, a region being one 256 m tile of terrain data; today
  it's 512, 2 x 2). The land is regenerated by `tools/generate_test_terrain.gd` at the new size.
- **Hearthvale in the middle**, the hub every trip starts and ends at. The other places keep their
  compass directions round it, about 1.5x further out than now: Greystone Quarry west, Frostpeak
  north-west, the Pinewood north, Stillwater south-west, Goldmeadow south-east, the road east
  (the trader, the market) east.
- **The Clearing** sits at the village edge, a short lane out: you start there by the pond with
  your tent, your home on the lane between it and the green.
- **The places grow a little:** the Pinewood and Goldmeadow about 1.5x (more forest, more fields);
  Stillwater, the quarry (its rings and the mine under it are built to measure) and Frostpeak keep
  their size and shape.
- **Getting about on foot:** gravel roads from the green to each place, footpaths and stiles
  (steps over a wall) across the fields, a signpost at every fork. No cart or horse; the far edge
  is about a minute's jog from the green.
- **Biome cards match the ground:** `Biome.biome_at` uses the same warped shapes the painted
  ground does (today a card can come a few metres early or late).

## Hearthvale: a village green, about 140 m across

- **The green** (~35 m): grass, the well, benches, the noticeboard; the town hall, the tavern and
  the market stall face it.
- **Lanes from its corners** to the crafts, each set toward its work: the woodcutter toward the
  Pinewood, the fisher by the water, the prospector on the quarry road, the smithy on the main
  road. Room round every house for its add-ons.
- **Top tiers get roomier interiors**, toward the planned size (up to 10 x 12 m); lower tiers stay
  as they are, so growing a house still feels like growing.
- **More than the villagers' houses**, round the outside of the lanes:
  - *Farm buildings:* a barn, hay ricks, a granary on staddle stones (mushroom-shaped stone feet),
    a cart shed, animal pens.
  - *Stores and yards:* a storehouse, a timber yard by the woodcutter's, an ore and coal store by
    the smithy, a woodshed, stacked crates and barrels.
  - *Village life:* cottage gardens, allotments (rented vegetable plots), a washhouse with washing
    lines, a dovecote, a pump, a small chapel, a stable.
  - *Empty houses:* two or three cottages, shuttered or half-ruined, with a "to let" board: room
    for newcomers later.
- Walk-in where it makes sense (the barn, the storehouse, the washhouse, the chapel), closed where
  not, all dressed so the village feels busy.
- **Some are noticeboard projects** (like the add-ons, each with a small effect): restore the old
  barn, mend the granary, open an empty house, and so on. Which ones and their effects are worked
  out in phase 3; first guesses, tuned in play.

## The country between

- **Farmland:** pastures and hay meadows round the village, hedgerows and dry-stone walls (stacked
  without mortar) along the field edges, gates, a lone field barn.
- **Woods and groves:** copses of mixed trees between places, lone old trees on the hilltops,
  thickets along the creek.
- **Wildflowers and water:** flower banks along the creek and the lake, reeds, stepping stones, a
  small waterfall where the creek leaves the hills.
- **Old things to find on a walk:** a standing stone, a ruined farmstead, a shepherd's hut, a
  wayside shrine, a bench at a view.
- **Gatherable trees and rocks where they belong:** rocks on outcrops (bare rocky patches) on the
  hillsides, at the quarry and along the stream banks; trees in the woods, groves and hedgerows,
  the starter ones by the Clearing. A placing script works them out once from the land and they're
  saved into the scene, so they never move between runs.

## New places (dressed now, their uses later)

- **A watermill** on the creek downstream of the village: a turning wheel, the miller's yard.
- **An orchard** on a south slope by the village: apple and pear trees, a cider press.
- **A shepherd's hill:** high pasture with sheep, a stone fold (pen), the shepherd's hut, a view
  over the valley.
- **A hidden haven in the Pinewood:** a secret glade reached by a faint deer track and a gap in a
  thicket you'd walk past. A small spring-fed pool, a mossy ruin, old trees, wildflowers, deer that
  don't bolt, fireflies at dusk, a log to sit on. Finding it is a trophy.
- Each gets a noticeboard project where it fits (restore the mill's wheel, mend the orchard wall).
  Gathering fruit, wool, or milling flour for Rosa waits for its own chunk.

## Life

- **Animals, ambient only** (nothing to hunt): sheep and cows grazing the pastures, hens in the
  village, rabbits and deer that bolt when you come near (the dog chases the rabbits), birds in the
  trees.
- **Small wildlife:** squirrels up the trunks, bees round the flowers and orchard, butterflies over
  the meadows, frogs at the water, fish jumping, dragonflies by the creek.
- Each with its sound, near and far, in the ambience.

## Saves

- **Progress carries over:** skills, items, tools, village tiers, add-ons, projects, the dog,
  trophies, the mine. Your position goes back to the Clearing; dug and felled nodes reset (they're
  in new places).

## Art and sound

- **CC0 packs, picked on a page first:** barns, sheds, farm props and fences from the Quaternius
  kits where they fit; animated animals from a CC0 animal pack; animal and countryside sounds as
  CC0 recordings, picked by ear.

## How it's built

- **Two stops for you,** then straight through:
  1. **The plans page:** two or three drawn layouts of the new map and of the village green; you
     pick one.
  2. **The assets page:** candidates for the new buildings and props, the animals and their sounds;
     you pick by eye and ear.
- After that every phase is built and committed in turn, with one report at the end and
  screenshots. Windowed runs on screen 2 (muted) are fine for this pass.
- `tools/audit_world.gd` runs after the land, the village and the country phases: nothing
  floating, no walls you can't get past, villagers facing their work.

## Phases

1. **The plans page** (stop 1): the map layouts and the village green plans, drawn from the real
   numbers (distances, walking times, the quarry and the mine's footprint).
2. **The land at 768 m:** the generator at the new size, the places moved round the hub, the roads
   and footpaths re-routed, the creek, the biome cards on the warped shapes, the maps (world map,
   minimap). The mine moves with the quarry. Saves: old positions back to the Clearing.
3. **The assets page** (stop 2): buildings and props, animals, sounds.
4. **The village green:** the plan laid out, the eight buildings on their new plots, lanes,
   villager spots and routes, the Clearing at the edge; roomier top-tier interiors.
5. **The village fixtures:** farm buildings, stores and yards, village life, empty houses; the
   walk-in ones furnished; their noticeboard projects.
6. **The country:** farmland, hedges and walls, woods and groves, wildflowers and water, the old
   things; gatherable trees and rocks placed where they belong.
7. **The new places:** the watermill, the orchard, the shepherd's hill, the hidden haven (its
   trophy); their projects.
8. **Life:** the animals and their simple ways (graze, wander, bolt), the small wildlife, their
   sounds in the ambience.
9. **Checks and pictures:** the audit, every test, a screenshot tour, the README, docs/TODO.md, this
   plan "as built".
