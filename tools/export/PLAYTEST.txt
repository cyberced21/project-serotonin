Project serotonin - playtest build
==================================

Unzip the whole folder somewhere, then run ProjectSerotonin.exe.
Keep libterrain.windows.release.x86_64.dll next to the exe: the game needs it.

Windows may warn that the app is unrecognised (it isn't signed):
click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Needs a graphics card with Vulkan (most cards from the last ~8 years).

Controls
--------
WASD / mouse      move / look
Shift             sprint (air dash in the air)
Space             jump / mantle / wall run
Ctrl or C         crouch / slide
Left mouse or E   gather (axe, pickaxe, sickle), thrust the spear, cast the rod (hold, let go)
                  and pull when a fish takes the float
Q                 swap spear / rod
E                 talk to a villager (Greta at the smithy forges tools and resets talents)
K                 skills: levels, XP, perks, talents
M                 world map (wheel zoom, drag to pan, Space finds you)
Esc               pause menu (skills, settings, controls, save and quit)

Levelling: every level gives a small perk; at 10, 20, 30 and 40 you choose a talent.
Woodcutting's talents work now; the other skills' are still to come.

Debug menu (left on for testing)
--------------------------------
F1                debug menu: items, skill levels, tools, teleports, fly mode, time of day...
F6 / F7 / F8      skip 3 hours / 50 of every item / upgrade every building
F9 / F10          give XP / reset save

Saves live in %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Project serotonin\worlds

Found a bug? Tell me what you were doing, and send the log:
